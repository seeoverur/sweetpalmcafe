Our Story

About Sweet Palm Cafe

Sweet Palm Cafe is a food-first cafe in Downtown Charleston serving breakfast, brunch, and lunch at 471 King Street. Our menu is built around hearty breakfast burritos, chicken caesar wraps, breakfast sandwiches, pressed toasties, and pastries baked fresh in-house every morning.

We're named after the sweetgrass baskets that have been hand-woven in the Lowcountry for generations — a craft built on patience and making things one at a time. That's how we treat food. Nothing is pre-packaged, and nothing sits under a heat lamp. Your order is made when you place it.

Part of the Sweet Palm Family

Sweet Palm Cafe is the food-focused branch of Sweet Palm Coffee. While Sweet Palm built its name on handcrafted espresso and cafe drinks, Sweet Grass puts the kitchen front and center.

Visit Us

We're open daily in the heart of the Charleston peninsula, serving King Street, the Historic District, the College of Charleston, and the surrounding downtown neighborhoods. Walk in any time, or order ahead for pickup and your food is ready when you are.
