Our Story

Named for the Grass That Made Charleston Famous

Sweet Palm Cafe is rooted in the same values that define the best of Charleston hospitality — patience, real hands-on work, and pride in making something one at a time.

"Every burrito gets rolled like it's the only one we're making today. That's the Sweet Palm way."

Sweet Palm Cafe is the food-focused branch of the Sweet Palm Coffee family. Where Sweet Palm built its name on handcrafted espresso, Sweet Palm Cafe puts the kitchen front and center — breakfast burritos, caesar wraps, breakfast sandwiches, toasties, and pastries baked fresh every morning at 471 King Street in Downtown Charleston.

Part of the Family

The Sweet Palm Family

Sweet Palm Cafe shares a kitchen, a King Street address, and a standard of quality with Sweet Palm Coffee — Downtown Charleston's favorite spot for artisan coffee and cafe drinks.
